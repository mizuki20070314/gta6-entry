# SUNSET COAST

<!-- An original Pacificora crime adventure made for the make.fun community. -->
<!-- The badge destinations below are part of the bounty entry metadata. -->

SUNSET COAST is a top-down open-world game set in Pacificora, a fictional city of
sunlit avenues, working docks, quiet beaches, and neighborhoods to explore. The
world and its characters are original creations.

Drive through live traffic or take the streets on foot. Steal cars, evade a five-
star police response, and follow Marisol's ten-job story as Rex Costa tightens his
hold on the coast. Shops, armor, saved progress, a changing sky, a minimap, and
procedurally synthesized sound round out the trip.

## Play

From the repository folder, start the local server:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Use WASD to move or drive, E to
enter and leave a car, the mouse to aim, and click or Space to fire. B opens a
nearby shop, F5 saves, Esc pauses, and M mutes the soundtrack and effects.

See the [make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).
